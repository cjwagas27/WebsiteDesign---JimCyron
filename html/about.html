Our Story

A small roaster with a big obsession

Two friends, one drum roaster, and a stubborn standard that never softened.

How it started

Behind a small shop, before it had a name

CY Café started in 2020 as a single drum roaster tucked behind a small shop in Compostela, Davao de Oro. Two friends — Carlo and Ynna — couldn't agree on anything except that most coffee wasn't good enough.

Carlo had spent years sourcing green beans from farms in Benguet and Bukidnon. Ynna had trained under a specialty roaster in Cebu. They had the obsession, the contacts, and a borrowed roaster. What they lacked was a name.

CY came from their initials. Simple, personal, and stubborn — like the coffee.

The journey

A decade, one batch at a time

2020

The First Roast

Carlo and Ynna roast their first batch in a borrowed drum roaster. 4 kg of Benguet beans, slightly over-roasted. They drink it anyway.

2021

The First Counter

A 10-seat space opens. No sign for two months. Word spreads faster than expected — the queue hits the sidewalk by week two.

2022

Direct Trade, Direct Care

First sourcing trip to a smallholder co-op in Sagada. Four more origin partnerships follow in 18 months.

2023

Training the Next Hand

A six-week in-house barista program launches. Every person who pulls a shot at CY earns it.

2024

Weathering the Quiet

Loyal regulars keep the lights on. The roaster keeps running. Bags go out by motor courier to the community.

2026

Still at It

6 years in. Same drum roaster, same standard, same two friends arguing over extraction time. 40,000 cups and counting.

What We Believe

Three things we never compromise on

01

Origin Honesty

Every bag tells you the farm, the farmer, the altitude, and the process. Nothing is blended to hide a bad lot.

02

Slow Craft

Nothing is automated past the point of care. Every shot is dialed in daily, every batch cooled by hand.

03

Fair Partnerships

We pay above-market rates, visit every farm at harvest, and publish what we pay. Transparency is the floor.
