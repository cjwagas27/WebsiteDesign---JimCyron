Our Story

A small roaster with a big obsession for the perfect cup

CY Café started in 2025 as a single drum roaster tucked behind a small shop. Two friends who couldn't agree on anything except that most coffee wasn't good enough. A decade later, that same stubborn standard shapes every bag we roast and every cup we pour.

We source direct from smallholder farms, roast in small batches every week, and train every barista by hand — because we believe coffee is at its best when it's made slowly, by people who care.
